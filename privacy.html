Privacy Statement

Crossing App (working name), made by Ember Creative Agency · Last updated November 27, 2026

The short version: we only use your location while you're on a trip, and only to show your boat to the people you choose. We don't sell your information and we don't show ads. Your boat details stay on your device, except the boat name, your first name and your route, which are stored with a trip while you share it and for up to 7 days after it ends.

What the app is

The Crossing App helps boaters plan an offshore crossing (weather, seas, tides and timing checked against the captain's own limits) and lets family and friends follow the boat on a map until it arrives. It is a planning aid, not a navigation tool.

What we collect, and why

  • Your location, only while a trip is running. When you tap Start trip, your phone sends its position every few minutes so the people you've shared the trip with can see where you are. They see your position, speed, time underway, arrival estimate and any check-ins you send, and your battery level where your device provides it (iPhone and iPad do not). Sharing stops when you arrive, when the trip's link expires, or when you tap Stop sharing. If you tap Allow for location during setup, your phone asks for permission; the position it returns then is not saved or sent.
  • When sharing starts and stops (preview version). In the preview version, your position is not recorded or sent until you are more than about 500 yards from where you started and, on the routes built in, past the start of open water. Sharing stops when you are within about 500 yards of the end of your route.
  • Home port (preview version). If you set a home port, it is saved on your device only: it is not sent to us and is not in any link. When your route ends near it, sharing continues past the end of the route and stops within about 500 yards of your home port. Your followers then see your boat to within about 500 yards of where it is kept, and those positions are stored with the trip for 7 days after it ends.
  • What is saved on your device during a trip. Your invite code, the current trip and updates waiting to be sent are saved on your device.
  • What is stored for a preview trip. In the preview version being tested, we store: boat name, your first name, the route, your planned arrival and overdue time, your positions at the precision your device reports, speed, battery level where your device provides it, and your check-ins.
  • The people who follow you. If you add followers in setup, their first names and any phone numbers you type are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us or put in any link. The app never sends texts or messages to them: when you start a trip, you send them one text yourself with the trip link, and a saved phone number is only used to fill in that text. We don't read or upload your address book.
  • Your boat and settings. Boat name, speed, fuel, limits and routes are saved on your device, and so is your boat profile in the preview version (make and model, length, engines, hull colour and home marina). In the preview version, your answers to the setup questions, the type of boat, cruise speed, fuel tank and fuel burn, and when you ticked the safety box are saved on your device as well, so you can carry on or change them later. If you add a photo of your boat, the app makes it smaller and saves it on your device only; it is not uploaded and your followers don't see it. Your safety checklist ticks are saved on your device too. None of this is sent to us or put in any link. If you use "Share my setup," those settings go into the link you send, and only whoever opens that link sees them.
  • Voice or typed setup answers. The setup questions in the preview version don't use AI: what you tap or type stays on your device. If a talk-or-type setup helper is added later and you use it, what you say or type is sent to an AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to fill in your setup fields. It isn't used to advertise to you or sold.

Who we share it with

  • The people you share a trip with. They see the trip details above for that trip only. The trip link doesn't need a login, so anyone who has the link can open it until it expires. Share it only with people you trust.
  • After a trip ends, followers no longer see any position. For 2 hours they see that you arrived (or stopped sharing), the place name and the time. Then the link closes.
  • Reminders. If a follower taps "Remind me", a reminder is added to their own calendar. It contains the trip's link.
  • People who open a trip link. The follow page saves nothing on their phone. It loads map images, so map providers see the follower's internet address, as with any web map.
  • Service providers that make the app work: weather and tide data (for example Open-Meteo and NOAA), map and radar images (for example Esri, OpenSeaMap and RainViewer), Google and Cloudflare (trip storage), and Anthropic (setup helper). Trip positions are stored by Google (the current version) or Cloudflare (the preview version being tested). They receive only what's needed to do their job, such as the area of your route, plus the technical details every website receives (like your device's IP address).
  • We don't use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

In the current version, trip positions are kept so you can see your trip history, until you ask us to delete them. In the preview version a trip is deleted 7 days after it ends. A trip that is never ended is deleted 30 days after its last update. You can delete a trip yourself from the app at any time. Deleted trips remain in our provider's backups for up to 7 more days. Settings stay on your device until you delete the app or clear them.

Your choices

  • You can use the planning features without sharing your location at all.
  • You can stop sharing at any time during a trip.
  • You can ask us to delete your trip data by emailing us. We have no accounts, so to delete a trip by email, send us its family link.

Children

The app is meant for boat captains and the adults who follow them. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, or add features that use new information (for example sharing a boat photo with followers, or satellite messenger tracking), we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change goes live.

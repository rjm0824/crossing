      Hour by hour along the route

      Seas = significant wave height (ft) and period (s). Wind in knots with direction it blows from. Model data (Open-Meteo / NOAA GFS-Wave, ECMWF); it smooths things out, so expect bigger sets.

      Inside the Sea of Abaco

      Inside chop is estimated from the wind (the cays block ocean swell). Whale Cay passage goes outside: watch for a rage when there's big northeast swell, and wait it out or take the inside route on a good tide.

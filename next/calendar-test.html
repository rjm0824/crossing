This checks whether a web page can put a reminder with an alert into your phone's calendar. The family page's "Remind me" button will use whichever way works. Nothing is sent anywhere; the reminders stay in your own calendar.

What to look for, for each button

  1. Did a calendar screen open, with an "Add" button?
  2. Before tapping Add, does it show an alert? (It should say "Alert: At time of event".)
  3. After adding, is it in your Calendar app?
  4. For Tests 2 to 4: did the alert go off 15 minutes later?

Share again sends the same message as Send link to family on the Live screen. Anyone with the link can watch until it closes, 2 hours after the trip ends.

What followers see

  • The boat on a map, once you are clear of the start (outside the Palm Beach Inlet, or past Green Turtle on the trip home)
  • When you expect to arrive, and how long since the last update
  • Your "All good" check-ins and the waypoints you pass
  • An overdue notice if you are not in by your overdue time
  • That you have arrived, then the link closes 2 hours later

No app or login for them. The family page cannot call for help. It tells them: if you are worried, call the Coast Guard.

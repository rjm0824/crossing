# Crossing home: test plan (Nov 29 to 30)

**Planning aid, not for navigation. The captain decides.** This page is for
learning what the app did. It never comes before running the boat. If
anything goes wrong, use the fallback at the bottom and don't fix it at sea.

Setting up (iPad on power, Auto-Lock Never, Hide my start on, home port set)
is on your crossing-day tick card:
https://claude.ai/artifact/2BNtygYUVDFkJmzBJnWBm1

**Why this trip matters:** it's the first real run, on the water with real
GPS, of the start gate ("past Green Turtle"), Starlink drop-outs,
keep-awake over hours, and the home-port ending ("heading in", "RJ is
home!"). Each line below is one thing to note: **the time, plus a word or
two.** Use the log page if it's ready; otherwise use Notes on the iPhone.

## At the dock, before you leave (Guana Cay)

1. **Plan page:** the answer for your departure (GO, CROSSABLE or NO-GO),
   the worst seas it shows, and its arrival time. (This tests the planner,
   not just the tracker.)
2. **iPad battery %** when you tap Start trip, and the time.
3. **Start trip:** Live shows "Waiting until you're past Green Turtle". Yes or no?
4. **Send link to family:** the time sent, and whether it arrived from your
   number, not "Mom".
5. **Planned arrival and overdue time** shown on the Live screen.

## Underway

6. **Sharing starts:** the time the Live screen says it is sharing, and
   roughly where you are (past Green Turtle?).
7. **Each signal drop:** the time it went and the time it came back, with
   roughly where you were. After it comes back, did the Live screen catch
   up on its own?
8. **Screen:** did the iPad stay on? Did the amber "screen may lock" note
   ever show?
9. **Battery % every 2 hours**, or at each named point, whichever is easier.
10. **Gulf Stream:** what the seas and wind actually felt like, against
    what the Plan page said. One line is enough ("4 to 5, short and steep;
    plan said 3").
11. **Any check-in you send** ("All good") and the time.

## Coming in (Palm Beach)

12. **Past the inlet:** the time. Family should see "RJ has passed Palm
    Beach Inlet and is heading in."
13. **At the dock:** the time the trip ends by itself (within about 500
    yards of the home port). Family should see "✓ RJ is home!" with the map.
14. **iPad battery %** at the end.
15. **Screenshots** of the Live screen at the end. Ask Haile or Mom for one
    of the family page.
16. **Don't delete the trip** until family have seen "RJ is home!" and
    you've taken the screenshots.

## Family side (text to send Haile and Mom before you leave)

> Tomorrow you'll get a link to follow the boat home. No app needed. If
> you can, jot down: when the link arrived; whether the boat moved every few
> minutes; any time it seemed stuck for more than 30 minutes (and when);
> whether you saw "heading in" and "RJ is home!"; and anything confusing.
> A screenshot at the end would help. Thank you!

## If something goes wrong

No code fixes on the water. The fallback needs none: the text with your
planned arrival and overdue time, and Apple Find My. Note what happened and
the time, then carry on.

## Debrief, Dec 1 (Sonnet 5.5)

Bring the notes, screenshots and battery numbers. Questions:

- Did sharing start in the right place? Did it end at the dock?
- Each gap: how long, where, and did everything catch up afterwards?
- Battery used per hour. Did the screen stay on the whole way?
- Did family see "heading in" and "RJ is home!" at the right times?
  Anything confusing for them?
- Plan page against reality: was the answer right? Were the seas and
  timing close?
- What, if anything, should change before friends use it?

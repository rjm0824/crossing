# Crossing App feature map (text summary, Nov 17, 2026)

Summary of the Feature Map whiteboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/3YYZzkcicwnENCVUT4rVFh. Where items conflict, the Nov 17 decisions (and `docs/HANDOFF.md`) win.

Legend: **Live** = in today's page · **MVP** = friends version / initial build · **Next** · **Paid** = paid beta or App Store · **Later** · **No** = not doing.

## 1. Set up (once)
- Setup screen: boat (speed, tank, burn), limits (seas, wind, north-wind rule), float plan contacts. **MVP**
- Guided voice/typed setup chat, 7 questions, Claude fills the fields, captain OKs. **MVP**
- Welcome copy B1; safety tick box with Terms and Privacy. **MVP**
- Boat type question + boat photo upload. **MVP** · Photo → line drawing (free sketch filter first, AI later). **Later**
- Route presets (West Palm → Abaco via Green Turtle; Lauderdale or Miami → Bimini) and tap your own points; trip-home flip (**Live**). **MVP**
- GPX import. **Next** · ActiveCaptain link. **Later** (legal check first) · Route library (South Florida ↔ Bahamas first, then opt-in captain routes). **Later**
- Share my setup link that pre-fills a friend's copy. **MVP**

## 2. Decide: go or wait
- Go / crossable / no-go vs your limits; north-wind-in-the-Stream flag. **Live**
- Best departure time, tap to compare, star on the best. **Live**
- Hour-by-hour seas and wind at each route point. **Live** · Water + air temp on the GO card and per point. **MVP**
- Fuel and arrival (sea-state adjusted fuel range **MVP**), sunrise. **Live**
- Inside-water check (Sea of Abaco chop, Whale Cay). **Live**
- Tides at leave and arrive points, curves with a live "now" marker. **Live / Phase 0**
- Route map + animated radar (radar licence to swap before selling). **Live**
- Competitor add-nows: compare forecast models, best day this week, plain-words briefing, marine warnings. **MVP**
- Comfort score. **Paid** · Weather routing / course to steer. **No** (navigation liability)

## 3. Get ready
- Float plan (route, ETA, boat, crew) texted to contacts; starts the family link. **MVP** · USCG-style float plan PDF. **MVP**
- Go-day checklist (ticks save on device, reset each trip); safety gear checklist. **MVP**
- GPX export for the Garmin. **Live**
- Documents checklist; customs helper (Click2Clear steps, copy-ready boat fields, CBP ROAM for the return; no API, captain pastes). **Paid / Next**
- Saved trips (Thanksgiving, Summer, Winter) + My Trips tab (upcoming, past, duplicate as new); trip wakes up with the forecast. **MVP** · Limits per trip. **Next**

## 4. Underway
- Captain tracking every few minutes; own private tracker per captain (shared tracker service, Option B); keep-screen-on fix. **MVP**
- Family watch link: any browser, no app; boat, speed, last heard from; battery and signal; expires on arrival. **MVP**
- Live stats + ETA; waypoint alerts; one-tap "All good"; tracking interval setting. **MVP**
- No-signal: store and send. **MVP** · inReach on the map. **Next** · background tracking (App Store), AIS. **Later**
- "Stopped unexpectedly" alert, two-way messages, marinas and fuel nearby. **Paid**
- SOS button (position + VHF 16 script, texts contacts, alert on family link; never replaces VHF or EPIRB); find my friends; multiple boats on one map; auto-start; ping on demand; AIS ships; boat instruments (NMEA); emergency dispatch partner; rescue dashboard; community. **Later**
- TracBack navigation. **No**

## 5. Arrive
- Made it + auto stop near the destination; kill switch or set stop time. **MVP**
- Overdue alert (check-in window, "haven't heard from" to contacts, last known position). **MVP**
- Trip logbook → now My Trips past trips. **MVP** · Trip recap card. **Paid**
